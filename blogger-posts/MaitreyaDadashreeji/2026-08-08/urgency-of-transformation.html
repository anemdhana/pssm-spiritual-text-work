Urgency of Transformation

A message for you — on why the world is changing so quickly, and the three simple, honest steps that can carry you through this time with grace.

Namaste. We wish you a beautiful, divine life with good health and happiness — especially for those who stood strong in this challenging time, and those who are spreading happiness, positivity, and peace. Thank you so much for that; keep doing this good work. Spread positivity and smile everywhere if you can.

Life never stops, so we should not stop either. We need to keep doing what we want to do, even seeing this current time. We need to keep exploring this life to accomplish our dreams. Nothing should stop our progress, our growth. So for a while you may take a pause, observe what's happening, change your lifestyle — but keep moving ahead in life.

The Toughest Test

All of us are very well aware that humanity is facing its toughest test ever. In the midst of this chaos, it seems we are not learning the required, vital lessons — things will never improve. How can we expect someone else to love us if we are not able to love the people around us? We want respect from everyone, but we don't have respect for others.

"If you want to see that change outside in the world, you need to change yourself first, internally."

If you want the best in life, give your best to life. That's how it is — so simple, not at all complicated. Start changing yourself immediately; the current time is asking for that positive change.

We have gone somewhere wrong in understanding our life, our nature, our planet. We have hit a nerve, evoking an unfortunate, fatal response from nature, and as a result we see no solace, but a worsening of the situation. To be very honest, friends, we sense and expect something more to come. So what do we do now about it? Do we keep doing what we were doing earlier, as it is, or do we change it — change it for the better? We need to move towards that change.

Three Simple Steps

Today we want to suggest and share some easy ways to bring that change about. It's up to you whether you want to apply them — but we know this will change you effectively; you will experience a profound transformation within, with just simple steps. Whatever we suggest, we always experiment with it ourselves first; we see the effect, we see the impact of it, and only then we suggest it. So at least try once. You will emerge stronger from any forthcoming impact.

1. Cultivate Spiritual Values

The first thing we can do is cultivate spiritual values — you need to embrace them. Any quality that attracts and expresses caring for someone, praying for someone, helping someone — anything that helps you increase love within you — just embrace those qualities and see the change within. It will strengthen your mind, and believe me, it will act as a protective shield in the time of crisis.

Your honest attempt is required, not perfection. If you think you are already a perfect human being, then you don't need to follow this step; we can go to the next.

2. Acceptance

Accept yourself as you are. Accept the situation as it is. You are not at fault every time — don't blame yourself, don't blame others. Acceptance will boost your spiritual growth; it will instantly uplift the level of your awareness. You reach beyond the realm of happiness and suffering.

You start understanding life truly when you feel a sudden loss of energy, or sometimes you're depressed, worrying a lot about what to do in life — no direction, no help, an overall confused state. Right there, in that state, immediate relief will come when you accept yourself as it is, when you accept the situation as it is.

When you accept it, automatically you will receive help, you will get direction, because then there is no internal struggle, no loss of energy. Help will be provided — but first, just accept it.

If you don't have legs to walk, someone will come and give you support. If you are in the dark, someone will show you light — but accept first whatever is happening; just be at ease with yourself. Everything is all right, everything is perfect at that moment.

3. Invite the Divine as a Friend

In this period of spiritual transformation, the most potent force that can firmly build your spiritual growth is the divine force — and that comes by connecting with the divine. Invite any form of the divine of your choice, and establish a connection — as a friend. Your divine will appear; he will walk with you and sit right next to you.

"The only way to connect with the divine is through love."

If there is anyone closest and nearest to you, it's your inner divine. So instead of searching here and there, search within — connect with your divine, and witness the change. We have this habit of treating symptoms, but we don't have the ability to focus on the cause of the situation. And the cause of everything that is happening around you is within you. The divine focuses only on the cause, not on the symptoms.

The divine light will tightly hold your hand to walk with you in this difficult time, helping you move to the next phase. So invoke the divine, be the truth, and free yourself. Those who are already on this journey with their divine friend will witness a shift in their consciousness. Keep flowing with your experiences, don't stop — there is more to come. The world needs positivity, the world needs love, and nothing else.

Your Transformation Is the Only Thing That Heals

Your transformation is the only thing that's going to heal everything on this planet. Instead of running behind mystical powers, mystical experiences, or spiritual games and shows — what is required, in the end, is your transformation. And that will happen only when you connect with your divine. You don't need to follow me or anybody else — follow your heart, follow your inner divine. It is so, so simple.

Take the step immediately, and allow things to unfold with time. Growth will be the only result. We are there with you all the time, to support you, to show you the direction.

A Larger Shift Underway

Our planet is in the process of connecting with the higher universal source. Many things will undergo change — in one way, we can call it a cleansing process. The atmosphere will displace and expel unsettled, dissonant, or disruptive energies, retaining purity alone — that's the direction from here onwards. Only purity, only truth will remain in the future.

"Listen to your heart, and your heart will share the truth. Nature is going to correct everything."

This is a shift in consciousness, and this shift happens once every 14,000 years. It's going to be a major change; all of us need to prepare for it accordingly. The human mind can't resist or fight against this change — the only way is to flow with it. Every day, try to become a better human being. If you keep doing this, automatically you'll be able to flow ahead positively, with fewer hurdles, less pain, and less suffering.

After correction and establishment, the long-awaited dream of a harmonious one world will become a reality, and the divine is actively working towards it. One day the divine will take direct control over its creation; many of you will experience this entire process of divine intervention. And that divine is not somewhere else — the divine is there with you all the time, within your heart.

So just be positive that something good is going to come for all of us. This transition might be painful, difficult — but with the change within, you will be able to flow through it easily.

My role is to help you pass through this phase with fewer hurdles — but the ultimate help will come from your own inner divine. So connect with your divine, express yourself to your divine; nothing else is required. Your divine as your best friend, throughout this process — we are there, all of us are connected internally.

I will come again; I will see you after two months. Through the divine light, I will be there with you. Very soon we will see happy, peaceful, better days for our planet.

Thank you so much. I bless you all.
