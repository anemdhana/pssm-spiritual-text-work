An Invitation to Come Together

A heartfelt invitation from Maitreya Dadashreeji — to set aside differences, join hands across spiritual paths, and meet the urgency of this time together.

Good morning, friends. First of all, congratulations to all of you.

I am consciousness itself — and so are many of you. I have done my part, and you are doing yours too. We all have our own gurus. So this is what I have invited you here for — for today's meeting.

This is only, and only, for information — not for any outside commitment. The purpose is this: we ourselves have built spiritual walls, we ourselves have created spiritual divisions. Even if someone stays exactly where they are, in their own place — all brothers should be like this, and every part of one single life should be like this too: united, when we all come together as one.

Who Is Adarshree?

So — who is this ideal one, Adarshree? He does not call himself a guru. He describes himself as a friend of humanity, and his purpose is that the mass consciousness itself should be uplifted.

He is a direct disciple of Aaru Baba Ji — between him and Baba Ji there is no difference. I know Adarshree, and I know he is a genuine master.

The purpose of this meeting is not that we abandon what we are doing, nor that we renounce the guru in whose refuge we currently are.

But — what can we all achieve together? What potential could there be, if we all joined hands as one team — without the differences of different spiritual gurus or names?

"Without the differences of different spiritual gurus or names — imagine what we could achieve together."

Today's Invitation

So I invite you — today, this is Maitreya Namita ji, and Maitreya Shrikant ji. They are very lovely, dear people, and a deeply devoted soul walks among them. They are very good friends.

I have also received an invitation from Shri Radha Shri — that you too should form a team, and bring your people along. There is a big difference between what one person alone can do, and what we can all achieve together.

Why Now

We have moved forward with speed, because we have less time now. It is high time that we help each other — this is simply information, freely offered.

Whatever query you have, ask it — find out what to do, what Dada expects from us. After that, whatever you wish. There is no self-interest of mine in this — but I am placing this claim on you, I am making this much of a request to you.

However important a position we hold, why should we not sit together, even once, in the ashram of Guru Dham? Attend today's meeting — just once. Will you do that?

Thank you so much. I am so looking forward to this, so excited, and so happy to work together. Love to all of you.

One more thing — whoever your close, wise people are, forward this to them too. Involve them as well, okay? God bless us all; let us do this together.

An invitation shared by Maitreya Dadashreeji
Srikanth and Namitha ji
