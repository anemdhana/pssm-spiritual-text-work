Captain Rita Surat, Gujarat, Bharath Desh

A conversation on why noticing "lack" keeps lack in place, and how readiness, inner purity, and the right use of Lakshmi are what truly invite abundance.

Tania asks

I wish to start a small pickle business. I have the potential, but no finance to start. That means I'll have to work even harder.

Captain Rita

The moment you keep expecting and saying "there is no finance," that lack keeps repeating itself. Energy is neutral — it simply mirrors back whatever you keep giving it attention to.
So ask yourself: in this moment, isn't what you already have enough to begin? The real requirement isn't a large sum of money — it's being in a ready mode.
Yes, money may feel less or unstable right now, and that's alright. If it means starting small or humble in the beginning, start small and humble. That beginning is not a shortfall — it's part of the process.
What flows to us matches the purity within us. As pure as our inner being is, that much comes to us — and exactly as much as is truly needed, no more, no less.
Ready mode.
Right amount.
If you'd like to go deeper into how this works, ask Roshan ji about vibration — how to attract what you truly need.
And remember Lakshmi herself: if she isn't used properly once she arrives, she won't stay, and she won't come again easily. The correct use of what we're given matters as much as the receiving of it.

Lack isn't solved by naming it louder — it's solved by stepping into readiness, keeping our inner state pure, and honoring whatever abundance already flows our way.

Only then does more find its way to us, exactly as much as is needed.
