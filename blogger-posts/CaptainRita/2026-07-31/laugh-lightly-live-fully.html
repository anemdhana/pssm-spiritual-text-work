Captain Rita Surat, Gujarat, Bharath Desh

A short reflection on identity, joy, change, and the quiet strength that remains even as life keeps moving.

We know who we are, but how do we make the most of our time by laughing, playing, and living fully?
Just as the stars and the moon hold their place in the sky, each of us also stands in a place of meaning within this world.
Not everything in life needs to be carried with seriousness. When laughter returns, many burdens begin to loosen.
Moving forward.
Happiness.

We will change, won't we? Even as the world keeps changing around us, something within remains resilient, adaptable, and alive.
